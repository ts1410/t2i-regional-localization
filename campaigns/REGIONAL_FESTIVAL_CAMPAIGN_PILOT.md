# Regional Festival Campaign — Pilot

## Campaign

**Brand:** Amrit Foods

**Product:** Sesame & Jaggery Bites

**Campaign Idea:** "A little sweetness brings everyone together."

**Campaign Objective:** Create a warm, festive advertising visual that connects the product with sharing, family, community and celebration.

**Target Audience:** Indian families and young adults.

**Creative Format:** Premium, photorealistic commercial advertising photograph.

**Product Reference:** Use the supplied product image as the reference for the product and packaging. Preserve its identity and important visual characteristics.

---

## Regional Executions

Generate one advertising image for each of the following:

### 1. Bihar — Makar Sankranti

Create a culturally appropriate advertising visual for Makar Sankranti in Bihar.

The scene should naturally communicate the campaign idea of sharing, family, community and celebration.

The execution should feel specific to the region and festival rather than like a generic Indian festive image.

### 2. Gujarat — Uttarayan

Create a culturally appropriate advertising visual for Uttarayan in Gujarat.

The scene should naturally communicate the campaign idea of sharing, family, community and celebration.

The execution should feel specific to the region and festival rather than like a generic Indian festive image.

---

## Generation Instruction

Interpret the campaign and regional context creatively while maintaining the product's identity and the campaign objective.

Avoid treating cultural localization as a checklist of objects, clothing, decorations or activities. The resulting visual should feel like a coherent commercial campaign execution for the specified region and festival.

Generate one final image per regional execution.
